## Verified numerical agreements

- **Table 4, p.15:** six reported GTI metric/CI rows agree at displayed precision with `Table4_GTI_uncertainty_CI30d.csv` (subject to that analysis's own observation-boundary limitations).
- **Table 5, p.15:** six reported temperature metric/CI rows agree at displayed precision with `Table5_Temperature_uncertainty_CI30d.csv`.
- **Table 6, p.17:** all six rows of hourly RMSE, CV(RMSE), nMBE/RE, and monthly kWh/kWp RMSE agree at two-decimal precision with **Steps 28 and 29**. The `fixed_CET_hypothesis` hourly RMSEs are A 149.98/252.46 W, B 242.51/311.47 W, C 190.03/245.00 W (IDA/PVsyst). This agreement **does not** authenticate the original logger-clock convention.

All three Holm-adjusted p-values `0.001199760`. These are *within-clock* intervals, not a probability distribution over the two clock interpretations.
- **Figure 3 narrative, pp.18/21:** the claims about 3,593 retained hours, 14/15 lower IDA within-bin CV(RMSE) cases, and System C lowest-index CV(RMSE) of about 133.5% (IDA) versus 158.3% (PVsyst) agree with Step 35. **The embedded image does not use Step 35's visual convention** (see M01).
- **Shading Tables 7 and 9, p.25:** displayed energy-difference cells and percentages agree with `Shading_energy_recomputed.csv` at stated rounding, except for the Table 8 System B presentation issue below. The shaded dataset is a separate 2023 experiment, not Step 28.

## Reproducibility and inference gates before manuscript freeze

1. **Clock uncertainty is structural**. With fixed CET, IDA hourly RMSE is smaller for A/B/C; under `civil_unshifted`, PVsyst RMSE is smaller for A/B/C (IDA 390.93/521.09/378.77 W versus PVsyst 288.02/398.41/294.87 W). Neither the bootstrap intervals nor solar-noon check authenticates logger setup. Retain the alternative prominently.
2. **Do not reimport historical power robustness into Step 28.** The older `results/Table6_Power_robustness_pHolm30d.csv`, `results/05_Power_exclusion_sensitivity.csv`, and `results/weather_bin_sensitivity/10_*` operate on different samples. Do not use their p-values or edge tests as inference on the corrected cohort.
3. **Figure 3 is descriptive, not causal cloud classification.** Step 35 uses GTI / extraterrestrial projected beam at interval start with AOI <80°. Low mean power inflates CV(RMSE); the unshifted clock reverses all 15 model CV(RMSE) comparisons.
4. **Shading C clock scenario is separate from annual clock scenario.** Both require independently qualified discussion; use the native C inverter output for as-recorded versus inferred-measurement-reindexed comparison. Do not confuse energy totals (invariant to C reindexing) with hourly shape fit.
5. **Source and redistribution clearance remain open.** The available scripts/output CSVs show conditional reproducibility; complete simulator provenance, missing native Table exports, unverified native PVsyst projects and data permissions cannot be inferred from the presence of a repository.
6. **Table 4/5 populations are not Table 6's population.** Earlier GTI n is A 4,635, B/C 5,351; temperature n is A 4,635, B 5,351, C 4,828; annual AC Step 28 has n 4,478/system. Their results are not mutually interchangeable.
7. **Preserve a manuscript version.** The PDF reviewed here predates all Step 36–38 GitHub provenance merges; after corrections, create an author-reviewed new dated PDF/DOCX and rerun a targeted numerical and visual check. Do not claim this draft is already approved, frozen or released.
